# Research decisions

- Decision: reuse observed source0.12.0 candidates and exact snippets from Mindfio
  v2/deep review, never rerun them. Rationale: owner forbids new benchmark generation.
- Decision: treating short ellipsis as a defect requires unresolved comprehension,
  not formal absence of an object. Rationale: existing genre/voice safeguards.
- Decision: punctuation checks consider relation and pause function. Rationale:
  Cosmos/closing-fragment and Tất cả/pause candidates expose the limits of byte-only
  preservation. No author-intent gold label or universal typography norm inferred.
- Decision: keep question IDs/primitives/Choice domains unchanged; update only narrow
  semantic recipes and canonical exclusions. Rationale: no observed transport/schema
  bug and no new Jev behavior experiment; review cues never authorize edits.
- Decision: distinguish true quoted speech/text from terminology emphasis. Rationale:
  prior runner froze all smart quotes, which is broader than canonical verbatim
  protection. Do not change the historical runner; clarify instructions only.
- Decision: suggest near-duplicate paragraph review within user scope and output
  contract, without adding a new auto-delete rule or a pattern from one publication.
