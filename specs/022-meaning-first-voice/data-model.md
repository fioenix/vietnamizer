# Working concepts (ephemeral, no persisted personal profile)

Source proposition→source span; actor/action/object, quantity/time, modality, negation, conditions, causal/temporal relation and emphasis. An edit may split/merge form while preserving the proposition set.

Style trait→example+communicative function+genre/reader scope+exception/uncertainty. Facts in a sample do not become article facts.

Candidate→source-supported / unsupported / unresolved. Unsupported or unresolved edits are narrowed or restored before voice review. Source omissions require equivalent expression or an explicit non-content exception; “vague” alone is insufficient.

Regression→ID, provenance, source, allowed/rejected candidate, rationale and validation status. Manual expectations are development fixtures, not new model outputs.
