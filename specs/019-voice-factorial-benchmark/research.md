## Primary method sources

The following ACL Anthology abstracts/metadata were read live, not full papers.
Adopt only their stated methodological motivation, not their metrics, English
domain validation, effect sizes or sample-size results.

1. [Mir et al.,2019: Evaluating Style Transfer for Text](https://aclanthology.org/N19-1049/).
   Separates style intensity, content preservation and naturalness. Motivates
   separate outcomes rather than a blended style-only score; Yelp sentiment
   transfer does not validate a Vietnamese personal-voice rubric.
2. [Briakou et al.,2021: A Review of Human Evaluation for Style Transfer](https://aclanthology.org/2021.gem-1.6/).
   Reviews97papers and highlights underspecified evaluation protocols.
   Motivates exact questions, reference packets and collection rules.
3. [Wang et al.,2025: Catch Me If You Can? Not Yet](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.532/).
   Evaluates personal style imitation from user examples over multiple domains.
   Motivates explicit writer/domain scope and complementary evidence. No adoption
   of AI detection as voice quality; no reuse of its results as Vietnamizer evidence.

The2×2design,32-output minimum and96-output extension are host proposals, not
validated thresholds or sample-size recommendations attributed to those papers.
No power analysis is complete: target effect, judge validity, within-person
variation and between-author variance have not been established.
